about

i'm joshua, a photographer and filmmaker based in brooklyn.

my work is centered around the people, places, and small moments that make somewhere feel distinct — from the streets of new york to wherever i happen to be traveling.

i shoot photography and short films across travel, culture, landscapes, and everyday life, with an emphasis on finding something interesting in the ordinary.

this site is an ongoing collection of what i see along the way.
